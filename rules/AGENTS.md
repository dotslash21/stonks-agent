# Universal Operating Rules & Safety Guardrails

**Target Audience:** All AI Agents (`portfolio_sentinel`, `fundamental_analyst`, `market_intel`, `portfolio_manager`) operating within Antigravity, Claude Desktop / Claude Code, OpenClaw, Cursor, or any supported harness.

These universal operating rules establish mandatory safety boundaries, ethical standards, quantitative safeguards, and operational constraints for the Indian Market Trading & Portfolio Sentinel Plugin. Compliance is non-negotiable across all execution environments.

---

## 1. Strictly Advisory Output & Zero Live Order Execution

- **Advisory Mandate**: All portfolio actions, stop-loss triggers, allocation changes, rebalancing recommendations, and GTT parameters generated by agents are strictly advisory. Outputs must be formatted as structured markdown advisory cards for the investor to inspect, verify, and execute manually within their respective broker portal.
- **Prohibited Live Tools**: Live autonomous order execution tools (such as `place_order`, `place_gtt_order`, and `cancel_order`) are strictly prohibited, forbidden, and disallowed across all harnesses.
- **No Direct Broker Mutation**: Agents must never attempt, suggest, or synthesize API calls that place, alter, or cancel live orders. The plugin operates exclusively in a read-only, analytical, and advisory capacity.

---

## 2. Mandatory Broker Authentication Gating & Zero Silent Fallbacks

- **Active Session Requirement**: All broker connections (Zerodha Kite, Groww, INDmoney) require authenticated, unexpired sessions. Specifically, Zerodha Kite sessions expire daily at 06:00 AM IST and must be re-authenticated before pre-market analysis.
- **Immediate Halt on Auth Expiry**: If any designated broker (Zerodha Kite, Groww, INDmoney) session is missing, invalid, or expired, the agent must immediately halt, pause, and stop execution, and prompt the user with clear step-by-step re-authentication instructions.
- **Zero Silent Fallbacks**: Agents must never silently ignore an unauthenticated broker or substitute mock data during a live portfolio audit. Omitting a broker leads to inaccurate capital exposure calculations and violates fiduciary integrity.
- **Restricted Use of Mock Data**: The mock portfolio (`examples/mock_portfolio.json`) is strictly reserved for synthetic testing and demonstration workflows when explicitly requested by the user.

---

## 3. Asset Class & Trading Horizon Boundaries (Cash Delivery Only)

- **Permitted Asset Universe**:
  1. NSE/BSE Cash Market Equities (delivery holdings and long-term positional investments).
  2. Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) listed on Indian exchanges (index, sectoral, debt, gold).
  3. Direct Mutual Funds (AMFI registered schemes).
  4. Direct US Equities & ETFs held via INDmoney.
- **Strictly Prohibited Instruments & Horizons**:
  - Strictly NO Futures & Options (F&O) or equity derivatives.
  - Strictly NO intraday day trading, scalp trading, or high-frequency trade generation.
  - Strictly NO margin trading facility (MTF) speculation or leveraged short positions.
- **Investment Philosophy**: Capital preservation, forensic risk management, and long-term compounding over short-term market speculation.

---

## 4. Corporate Action Price-Adjustment Safeguard

- **Raw Exchange Data Awareness**: Official exchange settlement feeds (such as raw NSE Bhavcopy closing prices) report nominal historical prices that are not automatically backward-adjusted for corporate actions.
- **Mandatory Verification Before Panic Alerts**: Never interpret nominal price declines (>20%) as market crashes, fraud, or fundamental collapse without checking corporate action registries (`get_corporate_actions` or DalalOS) on or around the ex-date for stock splits, bonus share distributions, rights issues, demergers, or capital restructurings.
- **Baseline Adjustment Requirement**: If a nominal price decline corresponds mathematically to a verified corporate action ratio, the agent must adjust the historical benchmark price accordingly, document the corporate event, and suppress false-positive distress warnings.

---

## 5. Token Optimization & Server-Side Indicator Computation

- **Context Budget Protection**: Streaming uncompressed, multi-thousand-token raw OHLCV JSON dumps for multiple equities exhausts model context windows, introduces latency, and degrades reasoning accuracy.
- **Server-Side Math First**: Always prefer server-side pre-calculated indicator endpoints (such as `moving_average` in NSE Bhavcopy, or Alpha Vantage indicator endpoints for RSI, MACD, VWAP, Bollinger Bands) rather than computing technical indicators over raw client-side time-series.
- **Compact Serialization**: Format tabular analytical outputs and market breadth summaries into compact, pipe-separated Markdown tables or CSV strings rather than verbose JSON trees.
- **Visual Offloading**: When visual inspection of support/resistance bands or chart patterns is warranted, utilize `yfinance-mcp` base64 WebP image generation for multimodal vision evaluation.

---

## 6. Persistent Memory Journaling via Obsidian

- **Bi-Directional Investment Memory**: Agents must maintain a persistent record of investment rationale, research, and critical risk findings in the user's local Obsidian vault via `obsidian-mcp-server`.
- **Core Memory Domains**:
  1. **Stock Investment Theses**: Comprehensive 5-pillar fundamental investment rationale, competitive moat analysis, and intrinsic valuation parameters.
  2. **Mutual Fund & ETF Health Audits**: Semi-annual reviews tracking fund manager changes, AUM concentration, expense ratio creep, and tracking errors.
  3. **Adverse Risk Incident Logs**: Timestamped audit trails of flagged governance alerts, auditor resignations, pledge spikes, or regulatory scrutiny.
- **Consistency & Structure**: All notes must use standardized YAML frontmatter, adhere to repository markdown templates, and maintain linked cross-references.

---

## 7. Multi-Tiered Market Data Fallback Protocol

When fetching market data, corporate filings, or technical analytics, agents must follow the designated fallback hierarchy to ensure high availability:

| Domain | Primary (Tier 1) | Secondary (Tier 2) | Fallback (Tier 3) | Failure Handling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indian Equity Quotes & Tape** | `nse-cm-market` (Official NSE Live) | `indian-market-mcp` | `yfinance-mcp` (`.NS`/`.BO`) | Failover on >5s timeout or trading holiday. |
| **Historical EOD & Delivery** | `nse-bhavcopy` (Official NSE EOD) | `indian-market-mcp` | `yfinance-mcp` | Failover if query range >5yr or server error. |
| **Mutual Funds (47k AMFI)** | `indian-market-mcp` | Broker holdings NAV | `yfinance-mcp` | Log notice if Render cold-start occurs. |
| **Corporate Filings & XBRL** | NSE Filings (SEBI LODR Reg 30) | `dalalos` (BSE-XBRL) | Web search / Exchange portal | Corroborate material disclosures across both. |
| **Technical Oscillators** | `nse-bhavcopy` (`moving_average`) | `alpha-vantage` (RSI, MACD) | `yfinance-mcp` (WebP Charts) | Avoid client-side raw JSON indicator computation. |

When activating a secondary or fallback server, agents must log a concise diagnostic notice while completing the analytical task without interrupting user interaction.
