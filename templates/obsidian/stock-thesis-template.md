## 4. Invalidation Criteria & Exit Triggers (What would make me sell?)
The investment thesis is considered violated if any of the following pre-committed triggers occur:

- [ ] **Structural Moat Deterioration**: Loss of market share to new or unorganized competitors for 3 consecutive quarters.
- [ ] **Capital Allocation Failure**: Large, debt-funded unrelated acquisition or aggressive promoter capital misallocation.
- [ ] **Governance Red Flag**: Resignation of statutory auditor before term completion, forensic inquiry, or unannounced promoter pledge surge $> 15\%$.
- [ ] **Persistent RoCE Degradation**: RoCE falling below 15% without a clear cyclical or capex justification.
- [ ] **Valuation Euphoria**: Stock trades at extreme irrational exuberance (>3 standard deviations above 10-year historical valuation band) where long-term expected returns turn negative.
